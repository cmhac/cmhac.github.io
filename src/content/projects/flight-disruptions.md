title: Flight disruptions
note: How often U.S. flights are delayed or cancelled for mechanical reasons.
kind: Reporting and analysis
order: 4
---

The idea came from sitting on a plane that wasn't going anywhere because of a broken door handle. Airlines report delay causes to the government, so the obvious next question was how often mechanical problems ground flights, and whether that's getting worse.

The analysis uses Bureau of Transportation Statistics on-time performance data, which breaks delays and cancellations into cause categories by carrier and route.
