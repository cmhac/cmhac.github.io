A long-established Washington Post tool that I maintain. It powers automated alerts into the newsroom when an incident meets the tracker's criteria, and feeds a public tracker and interactive page.

Maintenance work here is mostly about reliability and definitions: keeping ingestion running, keeping the criteria consistent over time, and making sure what reporters get in an alert is something they can act on immediately.
