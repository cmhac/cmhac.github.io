title: Supreme Court clerks and advocates
note: How concentrated the Supreme Court bar is among former clerks.
kind: Reporting and analysis
order: 2
---

A small number of lawyers argue a large share of Supreme Court cases, and a striking number of them once clerked there. I built the pipeline that measures how concentrated that bar actually is, linking advocates to their clerkships and to the cases they've argued.

I also worked on the data architecture for tracking the Court's emergency docket, which has grown into a significant share of consequential decisions and is far less structured than the merits docket.
