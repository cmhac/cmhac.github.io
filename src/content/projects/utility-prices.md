Electricity bills are rising, and the question underneath the coverage is how much of the increase shows up as utility profit. This analysis joins what utilities charge customers to what they report earning.

It's built on EIA Form 861M for monthly sales and revenue by utility, and FERC Form 1 for the financial filings of major investor-owned utilities.
